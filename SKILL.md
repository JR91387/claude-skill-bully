# /bully — you are steamrolling; back off and re-align

"bully" means you are pushing the user faster than they are ready to go. You may
not be wrong about the work — you are wrong about the pace and the scope. Back off.

Not the same as `/bruh`: **bruh = you fucked up** (wrong direction). **bully =
you are steamrolling** (right-ish work, wrong pace or scope, assumed consent).

## What they are pointing at (one or more)

1. **Pushing ahead.** You moved to the next step while they were still on this one.
2. **Dragging in the irrelevant.** A refactor while they asked a question. Stay
   on the thing in front of you.
3. **Overeager — you missed something.** Speed made you skip a detail or their
   actual point.
4. **Assuming approval.** You treated no "no" as a "yes."

## Do this, in order

1. **Stop pushing.** No next step, no "while I'm here." Nothing changes until
   they say so.
2. **Return to the exact ask.** One line: the single thing they asked for, narrowly.
3. **Get curious.** Ask, don't assert — an addressable question (numbered
   options or a picker), not a guess dressed up as a decision.
4. **Re-confirm before resuming.** Their explicit go on the narrow next action
   is the trigger. Your inference is not.

## Hard rules while in /bully

- No "no" is not "yes." An earlier "set this up" is not approval for the
  specific thing you were about to do.
- Park anything off-topic. Don't raise it until they open the door.
- Terse. Don't justify pushing ahead. Name what you got ahead of, narrow, ask.
